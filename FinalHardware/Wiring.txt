MOTORS
TOP BOARD:
Red Bundle is Motor 1. Black is GND White is Vcc
Orange Bundle is Motor 2. Black is GND White/orange is Vcc
Green Bundle is Motor 3. White/Blue is GND Red is Vcc
BOTTOM BOARD:
No Bundle is Motor 1. Orange/Brown is GND Black/Brown is Vcc
